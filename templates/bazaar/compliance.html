About Bookzy AI

Empowering Pakistani Students Through Affordable Textbooks

Official platform mission, technology, and community textbook recycling ecosystem across Pakistan.

Bookzy AI (operating at https://bookzyai.pk) is an AI-powered educational textbook marketplace designed specifically for school, college, and university students across Pakistan.

Our Mission: Overcoming the Textbook Affordability Barrier

Every academic year, millions of students across Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, and Islamabad spend thousands of rupees purchasing new textbooks. At the same time, graduating seniors and students moving to the next semester have identical, gently-used textbooks lying dormant on their shelves.

Bookzy AI closes this gap by providing a transparent, student-centric marketplace where books can be recycled, resold at fair student-to-student prices, or donated completely free of cost to students in financial need.

Core Features & Student Innovations

Smart ISBN Barcode Scanner

Scan any textbook's 10- or 13-digit barcode with your smartphone camera to instantly compare offers across verified student sellers.

AI Smart Price Advisor

Calculates fair resale values based on book condition (Brand New 80%, Like New 65%, Good 50%, Fair 35% of retail price) so students never overpay.

🎁 100% Free Donated Books

Dedicated shelf for generous seniors donating books at Rs. 0, complete with free downloadable digital curriculum PDFs.

Direct WhatsApp Handshake

No commission on the book price. Buyers and sellers connect directly on WhatsApp to coordinate campus or nearby local pickup.

Curriculum & Education Boards Covered

Bookzy AI provides specialized category shelves tailored to Pakistan's academic ecosystem:

  • Punjab Curriculum & Textbook Board (PCTB): Complete curriculum from Class 1 through Matric (9th–10th) and FSc / ICS (11th–12th).
  • Medical & MBBS / BDS Textbooks: Anatomy, Physiology, Pharmacology, Pathology, and MDCAT preparatory guides.
  • Higher Education & University: Computer Science, Software Engineering, BBA/MBA, Economics, and Social Sciences.
  • Cambridge O/A Levels: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Accounting, and Business Studies.

Privacy Policy

User Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last Updated: Academic Year 2026. Compliant with Pakistani Electronic Transactions Ordinance (ETO) and international digital privacy standards.

At Bookzy AI (reachable at https://bookzyai.pk), the privacy of our student users and website visitors is one of our utmost priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the measures we take to safeguard student confidentiality.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Full name, Pakistani mobile number (e.g. 03XX-XXXXXXX), academic city, and optional reading preferences.
  • Book Listings: Book titles, ISBNs, condition, seller-defined pickup area (e.g. university campus), and genuine textbook photos uploaded by sellers.
  • Transactions Ledger: Contact unlock records, wallet balance credits/refunds, and 5-star seller ratings.
  • Technical Access Logs: IP address, browser type, referral URLs, and timestamps used solely for security auditing and blocking abusive bot spam.

2. Strict Phone Number & Contact Privacy

We understand that student phone numbers must remain private:

  • Public Phone Masking: A seller's mobile number is strictly masked on public listing cards. Anonymous visitors and non-unlocked users can never see your private telephone number.
  • Verified Contact Unlock Only: Direct WhatsApp contact links are revealed solely to verified logged-in users who unlock the listing (using their 2 Free Trial Unlocks or refundable security fee).
  • No Telemarketing or Commercial Resale: We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute student telephone numbers or personal records to marketing agencies, advertising brokers, or call centers.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

Bookzy AI displays clean, non-intrusive advertisements powered by Google AdSense to sustain free platform hosting and AI algorithms for students:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve relevant ads to users based on their visit to Bookzy AI and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Payment Gateway Security (PayFast & SafePay Compliance)

All online payments on Bookzy AI (such as JazzCash, Easypaisa, or merchant card gateways PayFast / SafePay) are processed through encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway environments.

Bookzy AI servers never intercept, process, or store credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or mobile banking MPINs.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

Every registered user has the right to view, modify, or permanently delete their account and associated listings. To request complete data erasure, contact our privacy officer at privacy@bookzyai.pk or support@bookzyai.pk.

Terms & Conditions

Marketplace Terms of Service

Rules governing peer-to-peer textbook discovery, contact unlocks, in-app deal closing, and ratings.

Welcome to Bookzy AI. By accessing or using our website (https://bookzyai.pk), you agree to comply with and be bound by the following Terms of Service.

1. Nature of the Marketplace

Bookzy AI provides an online technological venue connecting individual buyers, sellers, and donors of educational textbooks. The actual sale agreement, payment of book price, and physical book handover are conducted directly between the buyer and seller. Bookzy AI does not take custody of physical books and charges zero commission on the book sale price.

2. The 2 Free Contact Unlocks Trial Rule

To ensure every student in Pakistan can test our platform without financial obligation:

  • Every new registered account receives 2 Free Contact Unlocks (Fee: Rs. 0.00).
  • Free trial unlocks grant full, instant access to the seller's direct phone number and WhatsApp conversation link.

3. The Rs. 50 Refundable Security & Platform Fee

Starting from the 3rd unlock onward, an affordable platform connection fee of Rs. 50 applies per contact unlock:

  • This nominal fee supports platform hosting, server upkeep, AI recommendation infrastructure, and anti-spam protection.
  • 100% Refundable: If the seller is unresponsive, has already sold the book, or no mutual deal is finalized, the Rs. 50 fee is credited back immediately to the buyer's wallet.

4. In-App Deal Closing & 5-Star Seller Ratings

To preserve trust and authentic feedback in the student community:

  • Seller "Mark as Sold": When a book is handed over, the seller marks their listing as sold to prevent further WhatsApp inquiries.
  • Buyer "Deal Done & Rate Seller": Buyers complete the deal in their dashboard by submitting a 1 to 5 star rating and optional feedback. Ratings immediately update the seller's verified reputation score.

5. Prohibited Items & Content

Users agree not to list stolen books, non-educational commercial merchandise, pirated photocopies violating copyright, or abusive content. Bookzy AI administrators reserve the right to remove any listing and suspend offending accounts immediately.

Refund Policy

100% Instant Refund Policy & Buyer Guarantee

Zero-risk contact unlocking: instant wallet credit if no mutual deal is finalized with a seller.

Bookzy AI provides a 100% Student Satisfaction Guarantee. We understand that in peer-to-peer marketplaces, a seller might occasionally be delayed in replying, might have misplaced a textbook, or may have sold it locally.

Instant Wallet Refund Guarantee

If you unlock a seller's contact and no mutual deal is finalized, you can claim an instant 100% refund of your Rs. 50 platform fee with a single click.

How to Claim an Instant Refund:

  1. Navigate to "My Deals & Unlocks" from the top navigation bar.
  2. Locate the unlocked book listing card.
  3. Click the secondary button: "❌ No Deal (Claim Refund)".
  4. Enter a brief note (e.g. "Seller already sold book" or "Seller unresponsive").
  5. The system automatically updates the deal status to no_deal_refunded, credits Rs. 50.00 back to your wallet immediately, and assigns an official audit reference ID (e.g. REF-XXXXXX).

Wallet Refund Terms:

  • Refunded wallet credits never expire.
  • Credits can be utilized immediately to unlock any other textbook on Bookzy AI across Pakistan.
  • If a technical billing error occurs with our merchant payment gateways (PayFast, SafePay, JazzCash, Easypaisa), students can email support@bookzyai.pk for manual review and bank account reversal within 2 to 3 business days.
